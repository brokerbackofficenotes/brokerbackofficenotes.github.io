Free template: Per-Load Carrier Vetting Record

A plain record-keeping sheet. It does not tell you which checks are required. It gives you one consistent place to write down the checks you do. Spreadsheet with dropdowns, or a printable PDF.

What's in it

  • Load # and date/time checked
  • Carrier name, MC #, DOT #
  • Authority status, and where you verified it
  • Cargo and liability insurance on file, with expiry dates
  • COI certificate holder matches?
  • Who you spoke to
  • How identity was verified
  • Red flags noted
  • Notes
  • Checked by
